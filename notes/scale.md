# Scale — how big is big business? (scale.html)

## Quotable facts
- Forbes Global 2000 (FY2024): **$52.9T revenue, $4.9T profit, $242T assets, $91.3T market value (May 2025), 92.6M employees**. Revenue ≈ 48% of world GDP ($110.6T) — a scale comparison, not a value-added share.
- Median Global 2000 company: $13.0B revenue, $1.0B profit, 23,900 staff, 10.1% net margin. Mean revenue is $26.4B — the distribution is skewed, so use medians.
- Firms above each revenue bar (Forbes): 1,967 > $1B; 1,210 > $10B; 242 > $50B; **83 > $100B**; 20 > $250B; **2 > $500B (Walmart $681B, Amazon $638B)**.
- Wider listed universe: at least **5,201 listed companies sell more than $1B** a year (2.6× the Forbes count); above $10B the lists nearly agree (1,305 vs 1,210).
- Concentration: top 10 firms hold 8.5% of revenue, 15.8% of profit, **20.5% of market value**. Top 100 hold 35.4% of revenue, 43.6% of profit, 46.2% of market value. Half of all revenue comes from 208 firms; half of all market value from 126.
- Gini among the giants: revenue 0.61, profit 0.65, market value 0.69, assets 0.75.
- Rank-size: top-500 Forbes firms fit a power law with slope −0.67 (R² 0.98) — flatter than Zipf's law; across the top 5,000 listed firms the slope is −1.13, close to classic Zipf.
- **True top 100 by revenue**: 16 firms are not on Forbes' list (9 state-owned, 5 private, 2 mutual) with $2.7T revenue. **China's State Grid ($555B) is #3 on earth**, China National Petroleum ($402B) #6, Trafigura ($240B) #24.
- **Magnificent 7 = $24.5T = 16.2%** of the $150.7T value of ~10,100 measurable listed companies (2026 snapshot). 15 companies are worth $1T+, 12 American.
- Value bands (listed universe): Mag 7 16.2%, other $1T+ firms 7.8%, rest of top 100 18.8%, ranks 101–1,000 35.6%, the remaining ~9,100 firms 21.6%.
- US firms = 53.1% of measured listed value ($80.1T); Europe 15.9%; China & HK 8.0%; Japan 5.2%; **India 2.6% ($3.9T, 569 firms, led by Reliance)**.
- Median measured listed company is worth only $1.8B.
- Biggest employers: Walmart 2.1M, Amazon 1.56M, BYD 969K, Accenture 774K, Volkswagen 679K, **TCS 608K (world's #6 listed employer)**. Top 10 employers = 9.5% of Global 2000 staff. India-based Global 2000 firms employ 4.7M.
- Median revenue per employee $545K, profit per employee $50K (firms with 1,000+ staff). TCS earns ~$50K of revenue per employee — about a tenth of the median.
- Profit leaders: Alphabet $111B, Saudi Aramco $105B, Apple $96B, Microsoft $93B, Berkshire $89B. Top 10 take 15.8% of all profit.
- 128 firms (6.4%) lost money — $186B combined. Biggest losses: Porsche SE −$21.7B, Intel −$19.2B, Boeing −$11.5B, Warner Bros. Discovery −$11.3B, China Vanke −$6.9B.
- Blended net margin by sector: Technology 17.4%, Financials 13.4% … Consumer & Retail 5.5%, Construction & Real Estate 3.7%.
- India: 70 Global 2000 firms, $1.32T revenue (2.5% of the list), $2.35T market value (2.6%). Reliance is #73 in the world by revenue ($114B); LIC $107B. Reliance and LIC are the only Indian firms in the true top 100.
- **Company vs country**: Walmart's revenue ($681B) would rank #22 among 204 economies (between Poland and Belgium); Amazon #24; Saudi Aramco's ≈ Vietnam's GDP. Reliance's revenue exceeds the GDP of 137 countries; SBI's 121; TCS's 94.
- **Nvidia ($5.2T) and Apple ($5.0T) are each worth more than India's 2024 GDP ($3.76T) and Germany's ($4.69T)**; Microsoft ($4.0T) also exceeds India's GDP.

## Caveats
- Revenue ≠ GDP: GDP is value added; revenue double-counts suppliers' inputs. Market value is a stock; GDP an annual flow — say "worth more than India produces in a year".
- Dates differ: Forbes FY2024 financials, market value May 2025; listed-universe market caps are a 2026 snapshot (CompaniesMarketCap, estimate-grade outside the US); GDP 2024. Never summed across sources.
- Forbes list truncates near $1B revenue and excludes unlisted firms. Listed universe covers ~10,100 of ~48,000 listed firms (only 569 of India's 5,000+).
- Bank/insurer "revenue" differs from sales; their margins and assets aren't comparable with industrial firms.
- Vitol (~$400B often cited) excluded: only a stale Wikipedia figure in our data. SpaceX appears in the market-cap source as listed — verify before quoting.
- Revenue per employee flatters traders, distributors and holding companies with pass-through sales.
