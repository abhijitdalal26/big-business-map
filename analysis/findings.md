## Caveats

- Forbes figures are trailing-twelve-month estimates at the list's compilation date; they are not
  audited filings and differ from company-reported GAAP figures.
- Market values are a snapshot (May 2025), not a live price. The 2025 list's market values predate
  later rallies, so today's caps are higher for several tech names.
- Financial-sector revenue (banks, insurers) is not comparable to industrial revenue: bank
  'revenue' is interest + fee income, which inflates the Banking total.
- The ten-year series only covers companies on the 2025 list, so it understates true global growth
  (survivorship bias).
- China/Hong Kong are reported as separate countries by Forbes; combined they exceed Japan.
