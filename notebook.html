Every quotable number, caveat and story angle in one place

These are the notes from every page of the atlas, gathered for study. Search to find a fact, then click through to the chart behind it.

Suggested study path (≈ the arc of an ET-style explainer)
  1. Big picture: the whole economy first. Hundreds of millions of firms, most of them informal; only ~48k are listed.
  2. Scale: how big the giants are, how concentrated, and companies compared with countries.
  3. Geography: where corporate weight sits (US, China, Europe, Japan, India) against GDP weight.
  4. Sectors: what the world's business does, where profit is made vs where value is priced.
  5. Ownership: the state, family and co-op giants that stock markets never see.
  6. Stock markets: the exchanges, financial depth, and the trillion-dollar club.
  7. Startups & AI: private giants, unicorns, and the AI capital rush.
  8. Change over time: who rose and who fell in a decade.
  9. Close with the India angle that runs through every page, plus the caveats below (what the data cannot see).
