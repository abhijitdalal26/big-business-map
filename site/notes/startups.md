# Startups, unicorns & the AI race — writer's notes

Source unless stated: CB Insights Unicorn Board (snapshot, joins up to Sep 2026); valuations = latest private round marks.

## Quotable facts
1. **1,442 unicorns** in 55 countries carry **$7.35 trillion** of paper valuation. Median: **$1.7B**.
2. **Anthropic ($965B) + OpenAI ($840B) = ~25%** of all unicorn value. The top 10 companies hold ~40%.
3. Only **five hectocorns** ($100B+): Anthropic, OpenAI, ByteDance ($480B), Databricks ($190B), Stripe ($159B), about 36% of all value. Together they're worth as much as the bottom ~1,290 unicorns combined.
4. **85 decacorns** ($10–100B). 54% of unicorns are valued under $2B, and 283 sit at exactly $1B.
5. **US: 825 unicorns, $5.0T** (57% of count, 69% of value). China 170 ($1.0T), UK 73 ($279B), **India 63 ($144B), world #4**.
6. **San Francisco (229)** has more unicorns than any country except the US. The Bay Area has ~320. Bengaluru has 27 (after normalising the Bangalore spelling), ahead of Paris and Austin (24 each).
7. **2021 minted 433** of today's unicorns (30%). The trough was 2023 (61). Then the AI boom: 83 in 2024, 123 in 2025, **180 in 2026 so far**.
8. **AI-native companies are ~6% of unicorns (85) but ~31% of value.** The tag is a registry name match plus "AI" in the name, so it's conservative.
9. Anthropic + OpenAI (~$1.8T) are worth ~78% as much as all 617 non-US unicorns combined (~$2.3T).
10. **All unicorns ≈ 1.5 Apples** (Apple ~$5.0T), **≈ 1.9× India's entire stock market** (~$3.9T), and only **~4.9%** of the world's listed market value (~$150T).
11. **India's 63 unicorns ($144B) are worth less than Reliance Industries alone (~$171B)**, and less than Databricks.
12. **42% of Indian unicorns joined in 2021** (25 of 63), against 30% globally. India added 14 since 2024.
13. **Bengaluru hosts 27 of India's 63.** Top Indian unicorns: OYO ($9B), Dream11 ($8B), Razorpay ($7.5B), Zepto ($7B), CtrlS ($5.2B, 2026), OfBusiness, VerSe, ShareChat ($5B each), CRED ($4.5B).
14. India's sector mix is consumer (17) + fintech (16) = half. America's is enterprise software/AI.
15. **Sequoia Capital India (now Peak XV)** is on 16 of 63 Indian unicorns. Tiger Global is next (9). Globally, a16z (85), Accel (75) and Sequoia (67) lead, and the Sequoia family merged is 136.
16. AI registry: **173 model builders**. 45 ship open weights, 24 partial, 104 closed. China's builders are mostly open-weight (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax), while the US is mostly closed.
17. DeepSeek and Moonshot AI are each at **$50B**, the only non-US AI labs in the top 10. Mistral (France) is at $24B. India's Sarvam ($1.5B) and Krutrim ($1B) sit at the floor.
18. Time-to-unicorn: in the matched sample (116 cos) the median is 5 years. AI labs take 0–3 years (Mistral and Physical Intelligence in their founding year).
19. Forbes Cloud 100 (2025): the median company is valued at many times the money it raised. Zapier is $5B on ~$1M raised; AI labs are near 10× because compute eats cash.
20. Hugging Face: BAAI (China, 58M downloads of its top model) and Google (42M) beat OpenAI (18M). Openness buys reach.

## Caveats
- Marks are not prices. A valuation is the last small (often preferred) slice, and some are years old. Down rounds and below-mark IPOs are common.
- Survivorship: companies leave the board on IPO (SpaceX, Swiggy, Meesho, Groww, PhysicsWallah), acquisition or death (Byju's). Early join-years are undercounted.
- Country = legal HQ (Postman counts as US; OKX counts as Seychelles).
- CB lists only ≤3 "select investors" per company, so investor counts are understated.
- Never sum unicorn marks with public market caps or revenue.
- Forbes lists are mid-2025 (OpenAI $300B there).

## Story angles
- **"Two labs, a quarter of the board"**: how AI concentrated private capital, plus the circularity of hyperscaler/chip money.
- **The 2021 hangover**: zombie unicorns still carried at ZIRP-era marks. India is the most exposed.
- **India's exit advantage**: its biggest startups listed at home and left the board, which shrinks India's unicorn count but strengthens its market.
- **India's AI gap**: no frontier lab. Sarvam vs DeepSeek is ~33×.
- **Open vs closed as geopolitics**: China's open-weight strategy under chip controls.
- **The private layer is loud but small**: 4.9% of listed market value.
