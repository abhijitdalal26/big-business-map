## Quotable facts (with sources)

1. **About 400 million businesses on Earth (375–450M), most of them informal.** McKinsey for IFC (2010), via CGAP: https://www.cgap.org/blog/counting-smes-and-their-demand-for-financing
2. **322M+ formal MSMEs employing 705M+ people** (data to 2019). IFC/SME Finance Forum MSME Economic Indicators: https://www.smefinanceforum.org/data-sites/msme-country-indicators
3. **MSMEs are about 90% of businesses, 60–70% of jobs and 50% of GDP.** UNIDO/ICSB (MSME Day): https://www2.unido.org/node/7470681
4. **47,980 listed domestic companies worldwide (end-2024).** World Bank CM.MKT.LDOM.NO. That is about 1 listed company for every 8,000 businesses.
5. **Only 52.3% of the world's workers are employees.** 34.7% are own-account workers, 9.8% contributing family workers and 3.2% employers. ILO modelled estimates: https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/World_Informality%20Regional%20statistical%20profile.pdf
6. **57.8% of world employment is informal** (about 2 billion workers): 88.3% in low-income countries, 12.9% in high-income ones (ILO, 2024 modelled; same PDF).
7. **84% of the world's economic units are informal** (ILO 2023 statistical update, 2019 data).
8. **India: 87.2% of employment is informal** (ILOSTAT harmonized series, PLFS 2025). Brazil is about 35%, Mexico about 57% and South Africa about 41%. https://ilostat.ilo.org/topics/informality/
9. **India: 74.9% of workers are self-employed** (World Bank SL.EMP.SELF.ZS, 2025), against 46.4% worldwide and 6.1% in the US.
10. **India registered 173,712 new companies in 2024, a density of 0.18 per 1,000 working-age adults.** The UK's is 19.2 and the World Bank world aggregate 5.2. World Bank IC.BUS.NDNS.ZS / IC.BUS.NREG.
11. **Global VC was $512B in 2025**, the second-highest year after 2021 ($753B). The US took $339B (66%), up from 45% in 2019. KPMG Venture Pulse Q4'25 (PitchBook): https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2026/venture-pulse-q4-2025.pdf
12. **China's VC fell from $149B (2021) to $44B (2025); India's from $37B to $15B** (same source).
13. **AI took about 50% of VC dollars in 2025 ($211B of $425B) and 64% in Q3 2026**, up from 18% in 2023. Crunchbase: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/funding-data-third-largest-year-2025/ and https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/q3-2026-global-startup-funding-ai-billion-dollar-rounds-exits-data/
14. **Jan–Sep 2026 VC reached $679B, already more than any full year except 2021.** Q3 had a record 27 rounds of $1B+ (Crunchbase, 5 Oct 2026).
15. **Global IPO proceeds were $290B in Jan–Sep 2026, against $114B a year earlier, even though deal count fell (908 vs 922).** About $86B came from one June 2026 IPO. EY Global IPO Trends Q3 2026: https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/insights/ipo/documents/ey-gl-global-ipo-trends-report-q3-10-2026.pdf
16. **India is the world's #1 IPO market by number: 379 IPOs in 2025 and 240 in Jan–Sep 2026.** But India raised only about $12B in 2026, against $164B in the US (EY, same report).
17. **Global M&A was $4.6T in 2025 (+45%), the most since 2021's $6.8T** (2025 dollars). The largest deal was Netflix–Warner Bros. Discovery at $99B. XBMA Annual Review 2025 (LSEG data): https://xbma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/XBMA-Annual-Review-for-2025.pdf
18. **World stock market value was $151.9T at end-2025, after $23T was added in one year.** WFE: https://www.world-exchanges.org/news/articles/new-wfe-data-public-markets-post-strong-growth-2025-despite-geopolitical-instability
19. **The US holds 54% of world market value (2024)** with about 4,000 domestic listings, down from about 8,100 in 1996 (World Bank CM.MKT.LCAP.CD / LDOM.NO).
20. **Trillion-dollar club:** Apple became the first US company worth $1T (2 Aug 2018); Nvidia was first to $4T (9 Jul 2025) and $5T (29 Oct 2025); Apple became the second $5T company (28 Jul 2026). Nvidia peaked at $5.72T on 14 May 2026, and no company has reached $6T. 19 companies have ever crossed $1T; 2026 added Walmart, Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix, AMD and SpaceX. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_public_corporations_by_market_capitalization
21. **BlackRock manages $15.5T (30 Sep 2026)**, about one-tenth of world stock market value. SEC 8-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002012383/000119312526420979/blk-ex99_2.htm
22. **Norway's NBIM ($2.28T) is the largest sovereign fund; nine state funds exceed $1T.** Global SWF (Oct 2026): https://globalswf.com/ranking
23. **About 3 million co-ops, 1B+ members and 280M jobs (10% of the employed).** ICA: https://ica.coop/en/cooperatives/facts-and-figures
24. **The top 300 co-ops had $2.79T turnover in 2023.** Crédit Agricole ($107B) is #1. **Adjusted for GDP per capita, Amul (#1) and IFFCO (#2) are the world's largest.** World Cooperative Monitor 2025: https://monitor.coop/sites/default/files/2025-10/wcm_executive_summary_2025_en.pdf
25. **Amul (GCMMF) had FY26 sales of ₹73,450 crore (+11.4%)**, its brand turnover passed ₹1 lakh crore, and it is owned by 36 lakh farmers. https://agrospectrumindia.com/2026/04/10/amuls-turnover-tops-rs-1-lakh-crore-driven-by-scale-and-farmer-network.html
26. **Global FDI was $1.6T in 2025 (+6%); India received $38.9B (+44%) and ranks #11 among host economies.** UNCTAD WIR 2026: https://www.iisd.org/itn/2026/09/09/world-investment-report-2026-fdi-recovers-to-usd-1-6-trillion-but-concentration-deepens/
27. **Trade has stayed between 52% and 62% of world GDP since 2008.** India is about 46%, China 38% and the US 25% (World Bank NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS).

## Story angles
- **"The economy you can't see":** the Global 2000 makes the headlines, but the typical business on Earth is a one-person, unregistered shop. India is the extreme case (87% informal, 75% self-employed).
- **AI is eating venture capital:** 18% of VC dollars went to AI in 2023 and 64% in Q3 2026, concentrated in a handful of labs, while India's VC has been flat at about $15B for three years.
- **India lists more companies than anyone but raises little:** many small IPOs funded by domestic retail investors, against the US's few mega listings.
- **Fewer, bigger, more American:** US listings have halved since 1996 while the US share of world value rose to 54%.
- **Who owns the giants:** index giants (BlackRock $15.5T) and state funds (NBIM, PIF, ADIA) own more than individual investors do.
- **The member-owned economy:** the world's largest farmer co-op brand is Indian (Amul), and co-ops are missing from stock-market lists.

## Caveats
- No official global count of firms exists. 375–450M (2010) and 322M formal (2019, reporting economies only) are orders of magnitude.
- VC totals differ by provider: KPMG/PitchBook $512B vs Crunchbase $425–440B for 2025. Recent quarters are revised up.
- EY revises its IPO history; the 2021 figure ($453B, 2,388 IPOs) is from the Dec 2021 release. India's counts include SME-platform listings.
- M&A totals differ by provider (LSEG vs Dealogic vs Mergermarket) by up to $0.5T. The XBMA series is inflation-adjusted to 2025 dollars.
- The World Bank's 2025 market-cap and listing data show a break (India 8,510 companies and $10.6T), and India's listing count also breaks in 2020. The charts stop at 2024.
- The trillion-club dates are from Wikipedia and mix intraday and closing values. Eli Lilly and Samsung lack citations. Aramco's $1T date is set to its 11 Dec 2019 listing.
- ADIA, GIC and SAFE do not disclose AUM, so their figures are estimates.
- New business density covers limited-liability companies only; the US is not covered.

## Not covered / gaps

- No per-exchange IPO split (NSE vs BSE); EY reports India as one market.
- No asset-manager ranking beyond BlackRock (Vanguard and others not verified).
