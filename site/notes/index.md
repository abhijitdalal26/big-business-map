# Overview (index.html) + Company finder (directory.html): quotable facts

## Facts
- World GDP 2024: **$111.7T**. There are **47,980** listed domestic companies worldwide (World Bank, 2024), and the labour force is 3.70B.
- Forbes Global 2000 (2025): **$52.9T revenue, $4.9T profit, $91.3T market value, $242.2T assets, 92.6M employees**.
- Global 2000 revenue equals **47% of world GDP**. That is a scale comparison, not a share of GDP.
- The 2,000 firms employ 92.6M people, **2.5% of the world's workers** (1 worker in 40).
- The Global 2000 is only about **4%** of the world's ~48,000 listed companies.
- Blended net margin is **9.2%**. **128** of the 2,000 lost money.
- Revenue concentration: top 10 = **8.5%**, top 100 = **35%**, top 500 = **71%**. Just **208** firms book half of all revenue.
- Profit is more concentrated. Top 10 = **16%** of profit, top 100 = **44%**, and **147** firms earn half.
- Market value is the most concentrated. Top 10 = **21%** of value, top 100 = **46%**, and **126** firms make up half.
- Sectors: Financials take **23.5%** of revenue and **34%** of profit. Technology has **8.9%** of revenue, 16.7% of profit and **26.8% of market value**, the largest sector by value. Consumer & Retail employs **25.5%** of the 92.6M staff.
- The US has **612** of the 2,000 firms, **37%** of revenue and **55%** of market value. China has 275, Japan 180, India 70 (4th), UK 68.
- Private layer: **1,442 unicorns** worth **$7.3T** on paper (CB Insights). Anthropic ($965B) and OpenAI ($840B) dominate.
- 60 stock exchanges carry **$148.5T** across 9,778 listings. The listed universe has 12,512 firms worth $150.8T.
- The reconciled universe holds **15,175 companies** from 17,985 source rows. Sources disagree on 756 rows.
- 90 state-owned, private and mutual giants with **$6.4T** of revenue are absent from Forbes (Fortune 500 layer).
- Directory coverage: 13,239 firms have revenue, 10,730 a market cap, ~2,400 a profit figure and 7,695 a Wikipedia page.

## Caveats
- Never add revenue, market value and private valuation together. They are separate ledgers and different dates.
- Revenue double-counts across supply chains, while GDP counts value added only.
- Forbes market values are a May 2025 snapshot. Listed-universe caps come from the 2026 scrape. Unicorn values are last-round marks.
- Forbes lists Hong Kong and Taiwan separately from China. "Country" means where a firm is domiciled.
- The listed universe is complete for the US and partial elsewhere. About 11,700 listed firms have no sector label.
- The pack does not see small business or the informal economy (the bottom ~95% of firms by count).

## Story angles
- "Half the world's output, 2.5% of its workers."
- "2,000 out of 48,000": how thin the top of corporate earth is.
- "The 126 companies worth half of everything": market value is far more concentrated than sales (the AI and big-tech premium).
- "Tech makes 9% of the sales but carries 27% of the value."
- "The invisible giants": $6.4T of revenue at state, private and mutual firms that no stock screen shows.
- Finder recipe: India + not in Global 2000 + $1B+ revenue lists large Indian firms that Forbes misses.
