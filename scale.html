The world's 2,000 biggest listed firms sell $52.9 trillion a year, and the top 100 take more than a third of it

How big is big business? This page sizes the largest companies on earth by revenue, profit, market value, assets and staff, shows how steeply that size is concentrated, and translates the numbers into country-sized comparisons a reader can hold on to.

How many companies are really big?

Corporate size is wildly skewed: a few thousand firms clear $1 billion in sales, a couple of hundred clear $50 billion, and only two clear half a trillion.

How concentrated is corporate power?

Market value is far more concentrated than sales: investors pile into a handful of firms they expect to dominate the future.

Who employs the world?

The Global 2000 employ 92.6 million people — about 2.5% of the world's 3.7 billion workers. Size in staff and size in sales are only loosely related.

Companies the size of countries

Rank companies alongside national economies and Walmart's sales land between Poland and Belgium. Apple's stock-market value is bigger than India's annual GDP. These comparisons are vivid — and need a careful caveat.
